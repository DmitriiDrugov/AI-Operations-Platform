# Product Requirements Document
## AI Operations Platform — Wellness / Clinic / Hotel

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-04-23  
**Status:** Approved for architecture phase  

---

## 1. Problem Statement

Wellness-focused hospitality businesses (retreat centres, medical spas, functional medicine clinics with accommodation) operate across at least five disconnected concerns simultaneously: guest relationship management, clinical scheduling, accommodation, billing, and staff knowledge. Staff lose time context-switching between tools, guests experience friction at every handoff, and management has no unified view of operational health.

The platform replaces that fragmentation with a single operational backbone backed by AI-assisted tooling, while keeping the business team in control of automation flows and knowledge content.

---

## 2. Scope and Business Context

**Assumption A1:** The immediate target is a single-location (or small multi-location) wellness business operating 10–200 staff and 5–500 concurrent guests/patients. Multi-tenancy is designed in from the start but multi-tenant SaaS is not the MVP goal.

**Assumption A2:** The business blends accommodation (hotel-style stays) with clinical or therapeutic services (appointment-based). Both models co-exist in the data model.

**Assumption A3:** The business already uses a mix of email, WhatsApp, online forms, and referrals for lead capture. No single CRM is in place yet.

**Assumption A4:** Billing is package-centric. Most guests buy a programme (N days, M included treatments). Extras are charged separately. Identifying what is "included" vs "extra" is a daily operational pain point.

**Assumption A5:** Notion is already used (or will be adopted) as the editable knowledge base for SOPs, service descriptions, and policies. It is NOT the operational database.

## 3. User Roles

| Role | Description | Primary Concerns |
|---|---|---|
| **Guest / Patient** | Person receiving services; may book online or arrive via referral | Self-service booking, viewing their programme, messaging staff |
| **Front Desk** | Manages arrivals, departures, room assignments, first contact | Fast check-in/out, daily schedule view, quick profile lookup |
| **Therapist / Practitioner** | Delivers treatments; may have a specialisation | Today's schedule, treatment notes, AI-assisted session prep |
| **Nurse / Clinician** | Clinical staff with access to health records | Medical history, contraindications, clinical notes |
| **Operations Manager** | Oversees scheduling, staffing, and resource utilisation | Capacity, conflicts, workload balance, escalations |
| **Finance / Billing** | Manages invoices, packages, charge approvals | Package inclusions, billable events, invoice generation |
| **Admin / Owner** | Platform configuration, analytics, staff management | KPIs, audit logs, integrations, system settings |
| **AI Copilot (system)** | Virtual assistant surfaced to staff via the app | Answers SOP questions, drafts messages, summarises history |

## 4. Primary Workflows

### W1 — Lead to Booking
1. Lead arrives (web form / email / WhatsApp / referral)
2. Auto-enrichment and deduplication against existing guest profiles
3. Lead assigned to front desk or sales; AI generates a personalised welcome draft
4. Consultation scheduled (phone / in-person)
5. Programme proposed and accepted
6. Booking confirmed; deposit invoice generated
7. Pre-arrival communication sequence triggered

### W2 — Check-in to Check-out
1. Guest arrives; front desk confirms identity against booking
2. Room assigned; welcome pack generated by AI
3. Programme schedule materialised into appointments
4. Daily: staff notified of today's appointments; guests notified of their schedule
5. Treatments completed; practitioner adds notes; billable events logged
6. Extras tracked in real time against package inclusions
7. Checkout: billing reconciled; final invoice presented
8. Post-stay: follow-up sequence triggered; review requested

### W3 — Staff AI Copilot Interaction
1. Staff member opens AI panel or types a question
2. System retrieves relevant: guest profile, booking context, SOPs, treatment history
3. Claude generates a contextualised response (summary / draft / recommendation)
4. Staff accepts, edits, or discards; accepted output is saved with audit trail
5. RAG context and prompt logged for quality review

### W4 — Billing Reconciliation
1. Billable events accumulated throughout stay
2. Finance opens reconciliation view for a guest
3. System classifies each event as package-included or chargeable
4. Discrepancies flagged; finance approves or overrides
5. Invoice generated and sent

### W5 — Knowledge Update Cycle
1. Staff edits SOP or service description in Notion
2. n8n workflow detects the change via Notion webhook
3. Changed page content extracted and chunked
4. Chunks re-embedded and upserted into pgvector store
5. AI copilot immediately reflects the updated knowledge

### W6 — Management Reporting
1. Nightly job aggregates KPIs from operational tables
2. Report generated and posted to Slack / email
3. Anomalies (unusually low utilisation, billing gaps) trigger alerts
4. Monthly analytics available in admin dashboard

## 5. Domain Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Lead** | A prospective guest who has expressed interest but has not yet confirmed a booking |
| **Guest / Patient** | A person who has an active or historical booking; the unified entity across CRM and clinical contexts |
| **Stay** | A contiguous accommodation booking with a check-in and check-out date |
| **Appointment** | A scheduled slot for a specific treatment or service with a named practitioner |
| **Treatment / Service** | A defined service offering (e.g., Swedish Massage 60 min, IV Therapy, Yoga Class) |
| **Programme** | A named multi-day wellness plan bundling accommodation and a defined set of treatments |
| **Package** | A purchased bundle of services; may be tied to a Programme or sold independently |
| **Package Inclusion** | A specific service type and quantity that is covered without extra charge under a Package |
| **Billable Event** | Any service delivery or charge trigger that must appear on the guest's invoice |
| **Practitioner** | A staff member who delivers treatments; has a schedule and may have specialisations |
| **Resource** | A schedulable non-human asset: treatment room, piece of equipment |
| **SOP** | Standard Operating Procedure; a Notion document defining how a process should be executed |
| **RAG** | Retrieval-Augmented Generation; the pattern of injecting retrieved context into an AI prompt |
| **Outbox** | A database table holding events that must be reliably dispatched to external systems |
| **Upsell** | Conversion of a package-included guest moment into an additional chargeable service |

## 6. Business Assumptions

| ID | Assumption | Impact if Wrong |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Single-location MVP; multi-tenancy is schema-level only | Additional row isolation work needed later |
| A2 | Guest and patient are the same entity in the data model | If clinical and hotel are separate legal entities, the data model needs splitting |
| A3 | Notion is the source of truth for SOPs; changes are webhook-driven | If Notion is replaced, the ingestion pipeline needs a new source adapter |
| A4 | WhatsApp integration is via a BSP (e.g., Twilio) not Meta direct API | Direct Meta API changes the integration path |
| A5 | Payments are handled by an external processor (Stripe); billing module emits events only | If billing is fully in-house, the invoicing module is much larger |
| A6 | pgvector in Supabase is sufficient for the knowledge retrieval scale | At >10M vectors or sub-50ms p99 SLA, a dedicated vector DB (Weaviate / Qdrant) should replace it |
| A7 | LangChain is pinned to a stable release; agent loops are bounded | Unbounded agents in production are a reliability risk; all agents have max-step limits |

## 7. Risks and Unknowns

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| PII in AI context windows | High | Prompt sanitisation layer; no raw clinical notes in AI calls without explicit staff consent |
| Prompt injection via guest-supplied text | High | Input sanitisation; system/user prompt separation; output validation before persistence |
| Notion API rate limits during bulk ingestion | Medium | Batch ingestion with backoff; incremental sync after initial load |
| LangChain version instability | Medium | Pin version; wrap in adapter layer; unit-test chains independently |
| Package billing edge cases | High | Explicit inclusion rules engine; human approval gate before invoice finalisation |
| Staff adoption of AI copilot | Medium | Inline UX, not separate tool; start with read-only summaries before write actions |
| GDPR / HIPAA compliance depending on jurisdiction | High | Data residency choices locked to EU/US Supabase regions; PII encryption at rest; legal review required |

**Unknown U1:** Whether the business has existing patient records that need migration. If so, an ETL phase is required before go-live.

**Unknown U2:** Whether practitioners need mobile-native apps (iOS/Android) or whether a mobile-responsive web app is sufficient for MVP.

**Unknown U3:** Whether the business charges in a single currency. Multi-currency adds invoice complexity.

## 8. Success Metrics

| Metric | Target (90 days post-launch) | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Lead response time | < 2 hours automated | Timestamp delta: lead_created → first_contact |
| Booking conversion rate | Baseline +15% | Bookings / Leads per month |
| Check-in time | < 3 minutes | Staff-reported; dashboard timing |
| Billing reconciliation time | < 15 minutes per checkout | Finance time-on-task |
| AI copilot adoption | > 60% of staff using weekly | Copilot query count / active staff |
| SOP question escalations | -40% vs baseline | Support tickets tagged "policy question" |
| Upsell rate | Baseline +10% | Extra charges / total guests |
| Treatment room utilisation | > 80% during operating hours | Booked slots / available slots |
| Report generation time | Automated; 0 manual hours | Nightly job success rate |
| Audit coverage | 100% of write operations logged | Audit log completeness check |
